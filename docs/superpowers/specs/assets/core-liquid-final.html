Move with WASD or the arrows, or drag a thumb anywhere on the field. You fire on your own. Turn weapons on and off above to feel each one. Tap or press Space to go again after you fall.

The Core: polarity duel

A grey-box prototype with first-guess numbers, here to judge how the level plays. Every body in the Core is magnetised North or South, and so are you.

The rule

  • Opposites attract. They rush you and latch on. Your shots hit them for double.
  • Same poles repel. They hold a ring at range and shoot. Your shots hit them for half.
  • Every 15 seconds the Core flips your pole. Whatever is latched on is blown off.

The floor

Lodestone pillar
A fixed pole. Pulls opposite bodies in and pushes same ones away, you included. Herd the swarm onto it, or hold on next to one.
Polarity pool
Molten iron of one pole. Step into the other colour and you flip at once. A creature that wades through takes the pool's pole.
Nexus compass
The arrow at the edge points down to the Nexus, the boss below.

The creatures

Ferrite Mite
The swarm. Rushes and latches, or rings you and spits needles.
Needle Urchin
Keeps its distance whatever the poles, fires a fan of five needles.
Spinning Top
Its pole strobes every 2 seconds. Shoot it while it shows the other colour.
Ferrofluid Blob
Slow. When opposite it grows spikes toward you. Splits in two when it dies.
Dipole Eel
Red head, blue tail. Bites with its opposite end, turns round on a flip.
Lodestone Golem
The tank. Drags you in, or shoves you off and fires needles.

The weapons

Sling
Starter. An iron ball with your pole: double on opposites, half on same.
Gauss Coil
A slug that pierces every opposite body in its line and stops on the first same one.
Dipole Lance
A short two-coloured beam that always hits with the right end, so no penalty.
Reversal Pulse
A ring that flips the pole of everything it touches.
