# The Ophan (Book 3 final boss) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A hidden seventh Burrow level, `ophan`. It is a one-screen boss fight against the Ophan, which wears the six Burrow chapters as skins. It must be playable end to end on a phone behind DEV, drawn by the approved mock-up's painters, hands, stages, wipe and gauge.

**Architecture:**
- **Rules in the sim.** sim.js holds the fight's rules: `stepBossScript` dispatches to `stepOphanScript`, state lives on `run.script.oph`, events go on `run.events`.
- **The boss is an enemy.** It is an entity in `run.enemies` (the Kraken head's precedent), so the kit targets it as an ordinary closest-enemy candidate and the damage sites gate it.
- **One shared geometry.** Attack hit areas come from one pure module, `src/ophan/rig.js` (the mock-up's `OPHAN_RIG`). Both the sim and the painters import it, so the hit area is the drawn shape by construction.
- **Its own renderer.** A new module, `src/ophan/`, is ported from the game-tech build: px2d, seven stages, seven painters, seven hands, the wipe and the warm-up. render.js hosts it the way it hosts toon, ascii and liquid. render.js hides its own floor and crowd layers, pins the camera on the arena, and keeps the player, weapons, pickups, numbers and the hurt flash on top.

**Tech Stack:** vanilla JS, PixiJS v8 (the repo's installed 8.19), Vite (no top-level await; `inlineDynamicImports` unchanged), plain-node `assert` scenarios in `test/sim-test.js`, headless Chrome probes (`scripts/fx-probe.mjs`, `scripts/shot.mjs`), the S25 over adb.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-the-ophan-final-boss-design.md`. Its owner rulings table is law, plus the targeting ruling below, which Task 1 writes into it.

## Global Constraints

- **The spec's owner rulings are binding.** They cover:
  - **Run:** a normal run.
  - **Unlock:** beat The Core at difficulty 3; DEV opens it anyway.
  - **Crowd:** each world's own roster.
  - **Kit:** the fixed kit `shovel, prismShard, pickaxe, slagLadle, hotCoals, sling`, with `shovel` as the starter.
  - **Switching:** tap a band of the six-band gauge on the left edge (keys 1-6). Broken worlds are greyed and refuse.
  - **Mismatch:** the boss is fully immune (the immune ring); the crowd dies in any world; the boss's attacks always hurt.
  - **Attack pool:** d1 2, d2 4, d3 6 attacks; the laser from phase 2; difficulty capped at 3.
  - **Arena:** one screen, the boss in the top third, the crowd walks in from the edges.
  - **Old crowd:** turns in place as the wipe passes (same archetype, same health share).
  - **Hazards:** none.
  - **Kit firing:** always fires.
  - **Sketch arena:** paper floor, no crowd, gauge greyed.
  - **v9.0.0 sketch:** the six pencil attacks only.
- **Targeting (owner ruling, 2026-10-24): "closest enemy, always".** The boss is an ordinary candidate at every targeting site. It is picked whenever it is the closest, matched or not, with no preference and no exclusion. Mismatched hits bounce with the immune ring. The seal acts only where damage or a status lands.
