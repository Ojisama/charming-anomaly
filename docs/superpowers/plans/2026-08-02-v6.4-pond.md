Slice 3 of the early-chapters revamp train
(spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-01-early-chapters-revamp-design.md` §v6.4).

## The four items

### 1. BIOME_POND — own flora (render.js only)

Replace `BIOMES.pond: BIOME_GARDEN` (today the same object reference) with a real
`BIOME_POND`:

- **big**: reed at bush scale in dark murk tints, bush_a in deep-water tints, NEW baked
  `lilypad` prop. ⚡ The lilypad silhouette must be an IRREGULAR lobed pad
  (radialOutline-style rim perturbation, 2-3 uneven lobes + a tear) with wedge notch,
  radial veins, dark rim — never a smooth ellipse (the render.js:4397 "programmer art"
  rule; every baked prop perturbs its silhouette before detailing).
- **mid**: reed ×2 multiplicity (murk), mushroom (pale bog), cluster_a/b/c (algae tints),
  grass_c/d (weed tints), lilypad small. NO flower_a / flower_b.
- **detail**: pebble, scatter_a (pale algae dust), leaf (dark drift-leaf).
- **obstacle**: keep `clumps` + `foot: 0x243617`; mass tint shifts murkier.
- Tints authored against the EFFECTIVE floor (avg blotch × floorTint 0x66c2a9 over
  bgColor 0x2e6258) in the decor band ~1.19-1.57× (v5.6.10 methodology).
- ⚡ Ship gate: creature silhouettes (tadpole/amoeba/tardigrade) re-checked IN BROWSER
  against the new prop-dense floor — `scripts/obstacle-contrast.mjs` structurally cannot
  see a prop-only swap (its model excludes props by design).

### 2. Eddies — streamed vortices (sim + render)

`CHAPTERS.pond.signature` gains an `eddies` block (signature owns its numbers, like
strength/scale/drift):

```js
eddies: { cell: 1000, chance: 0.5, r: 170, pull: 30, swirl: 120, minDist: 480 }
```

- ⚡ Density is a DIRECT per-cell probability (`chance`), not an obstacle-style count —
  the count formula saturates at this cell size (prob ≈ 3.9, dead knob).
- `streamEddies(run)`: copy of streamObstacles' idiom — own cell size, obstacleCellHash
  with salts 11 (occupancy) / 12 (x jitter) / 13 (y jitter) / 14 (swirl direction sign),
  same `run._obstacleSeed`, same OBSTACLE_STREAM_RADIUS/OBSTACLE_DROP_RADIUS, spawn-ring
  clearance `minDist` from origin, ZERO `Math.random()` at step time (hard rule).
  Maintains `run.eddies = [{ x, y, r, dir, _cell }]` (dir = ±1 swirl sign).
- **Force folds into `currentForce`** (one function stays THE field — stepCurrents'
  player+enemy push, tideCarried clouds, and render streaks all inherit it):
  - squared-distance cull first, then per eddy in range:
    inward radial `pull · (1 − d/r)` — ⚡ NOT multiplied by `currentForceMul`
    (the escape invariant must hold under riptide; riptide doubles the ambient
    shove and the swirl, never the trap vector);
    tangential `swirl · sin(π·d/r) · dir · run.mods.currentForceMul` (0 at center
    and rim; provably contributes nothing to radial escape).
  - ⚡ Escape invariant (config assert in tests): `pull + 2√2·(strength·0.5·2)` <
    PLAYER.baseSpeed — with pull 30 and strength 55 that is 185.6 < 220.
- ⚡ `MUTATORS.sticky.exclude` gains `'pond'` (chapter-exclusion mechanism, precedent
  `exclude: ['beyond']`): a flat player-slow stacked on the forces chapter was the one
  combination that re-broke the invariant (220 → 187 vs 185.6, a 1.4 px/s margin).
- `state.js`: `eddies: []` init next to `obstacles: []` (unconditional — currentForce
  loops it every pond frame), doc-block entry for the array shape.
- Render (render.js):
  - ⚡ Decal = the gravity-hole's proven treatment, not a lone low-alpha twirl:
    pooled two-layer `twirl_02` + `twirl_01` counter-rotating at high alpha + a stroked
    rim ring at exactly r, per-frame rotation advanced with the eddy's `dir` sign
    (drawn spin must match the force's actual direction). Tint contrast-measured vs
    the effective pond floor; hue must separate from CURRENT_VIS teal 0xa8fbef.
  - `chapterHasEddies` latch beside `chapterHasCurrents` (render.js:11216);
    `clearEddies()` called from `clearWorld()` beside `clearCurrents()`.
  - Ripple trains gain an eddy bias: when an eddy is in view, seed the 3-streak train
    tangentially on its ring (respawnStreak(atX, atY) exists). Streak capture inside
    the field is automatic (streaks Euler-integrate currentForce).
  - ⚡ Enemy current-wobble (pond only): small rotation wobble on enemy sprites
    proportional to local |currentForce| — the swarm visibly reacts to the water.
  - Ship gate: eddy legible from OUTSIDE its ring at phone zoom (browser pass).

### 3. Tardigrade gains `phase` (cryptobiosis flicker)

- Roster: tardigrade `flags: [] → ['phase']`. Constants stay global
  (PHASE_SOLID_T 1.6 / PHASE_GHOST_T 1.0 / PHASE_GHOST_SPEED_MUL 1.4 — beyond's
  drifter adopts the same flag in v6.6). ⚡ Doc comment near the PHASE_* block
  recording the measured impact: ghost 38.5% uptime ⇒ ×1.6-1.65 tank TTK
  (panel-validated empirically; change these constants with that multiplier in view).
- ⚡ Render tell (bare 0.35 alpha alone reads as a damage-desync bug on the
  most-farmed enemy): ghost joins the status-tint ladder (pale spectral tint, ranked
  below stun/fear, above eliteIridescent) + a one-shot small particle at each
  solid↔ghost flip (per-sprite last-state tracking in syncEnemies).
- ⚡ soapTrail ghost guard: the elite trail drop site (sim.js:1179) gains
  `e._phaseSolid !== false` — the ghost contract is "eats nothing, deals nothing";
  an untouchable elite must not keep laying damaging pools.
- Panel-verified: adding the flag shifts ZERO existing tests (byte-identical suite
  output with the flag enabled — no pond test reaches the t≥140s tank threshold).

### 4. Augments — bloom/mine control identity

- `BLOOM_SLOW = 0.35`, `BLOOM_SLOW_T = 0.15` (config.js): per-frame pass in stepBlooms
  sets `e.bloomSlowT = BLOOM_SLOW_T` for enemies inside any cloud — ⚡ guarded by
  `!damageImmune(e)` (a ghost is immune to the zone like everything else);
  stepEnemyMovement multiplies slowMul by `(1 − BLOOM_SLOW)` while `(e.bloomSlowT||0)>0`;
  decays in the fearT/stunT/enrageT block; state.js doc-block entry beside stunT.
  Known ceiling (ponytail comment): movement state machines that bypass slowMul
  (dashBurst dash phase etc.) keep full speed — same ceiling chill has.
- `MINE_STUN = 0.3`: detonateMine, for every enemy in radius —
  ⚡ `if (!damageImmune(e)) e.stunT = Math.max(e.stunT || 0, MINE_STUN)`.
  Render/SFX complete already (stun tint + orbiting stars).
- `WEAPON_MODS.bloom.tideCarried`
  `{ name: 'Tide-Carried', desc: 'clouds ride the current, ticking harder', icon: '🌊',
  base: 1, kind: 'flat' }` — with picks: stepBlooms drifts each cloud by
  `currentForce(bl.x, bl.y) · dt · picks` and ticks `× (1 + TIDE_DMG_BONUS · picks)`.
  ⚡ `TIDE_DMG_BONUS = 0.35` (0.25 was below virulent's single-pick floor — the themed
  pick must not be a strict damage downgrade). fr.js gains BOTH the name and desc keys
  (pattern: all 6 existing bloom mods have both). Render cue: a tideCarried cloud
  inside an eddy shifts its puff tint toward cyan (the orbit is an observable event).
- ⚡ Flagella knockback 45/50/56/62/70 → **62/68/76/85/95** (L1-L5): panel probe
  measured 63% higher damage intake kiting pond vs garden at d3, entirely hit-frequency
  (melee proximity); this discharges the v6.3.4 deferred melee check.

## Panel decisions of record

- Riptide desc stays truthful: ambient ×2 and swirl ×2 are "currents shove twice as
  hard"; the pull exemption is the invariant, not a lie.
- Phase economy hit (tank payout delayed ~60% into the dmgScale ramp) accepted —
  ghosted tank is also harmless; walk-away philosophy holds.
- Pond d1 likely reads slightly easier than body d1 (player CC baseline vs harmless-38%
  tank) — accepted for chapter 2 accessibility; kiting probe will quantify.
- Frozen+ghost knockback-through-wall edge accepted as-is; the phase contract test
  locks the primary contract (solid↔ghost cycle, ghost immune/harmless/wall-passing).
- Fun critic's player-anchored wake streak: SKIPPED (pond player already carries the
  flagellum tail; 40 field streaks + enemy wobble carry the read) — revisit on playtest.

## Tests (Run NN, appended at END of test/sim-test.js)

- NN.a eddy streaming determinism: same seed ⇒ same run.eddies; zero Math.random
  consumed by streamEddies (call-count guard); minDist honored; drop radius honored.
- NN.b force contract: planted eddy — inward component at d=r/2 matches
  pull·(1−d/r); tangential matches swirl·sin(π·d/r)·dir; pull term unchanged under
  mods.currentForceMul=2 while swirl doubles.
- NN.c escape config assert: pull + 2√2·(strength·0.5·2) < PLAYER.baseSpeed; plus
  MUTATORS.sticky.exclude includes 'pond'.
- NN.d phase contract: tardigrade cycles _phaseSolid; ghosted takes no damage, deals
  no contact damage, passes an obstacle; soapTrail elite drops no pool while ghosted.
- NN.e mine stun: detonation sets stunT = MINE_STUN on solid enemies, not on ghosted.
- NN.f bloom slow: two planted enemies in/out of a cloud — inside moves slower by
  (1−BLOOM_SLOW); bloomSlowT decays after leaving.
- NN.g tideCarried: modded cloud drifts along currentForce and ticks ×(1+bonus);
  unmodded cloud stays put.
